# CargoFlow
### Less Delays. More Flow.

**From port to warehouse, we keep your cargo moving.**

## About CargoFlow

CargoFlow is a proposed cargo visibility and exception-management platform designed for businesses moving goods through Nigeria's maritime logistics ecosystem.

Our central insight is simple:

**The cargo is moving. The information isn't.**

A single cargo journey can involve shipping lines, port terminals, Customs, clearing agents, truck operators and warehouses. Each participant manages part of the journey, often using separate systems and communication channels.

CargoFlow is designed to bring relevant operational information together around the shipment itself.

## The Problem

Fragmented logistics information makes it difficult for cargo owners and logistics businesses to understand shipment progress, identify delays, determine the cause of a problem and know who needs to act next.

When operational exceptions are discovered late, businesses may face avoidable demurrage, storage charges, truck detention and inventory disruptions.

## Our Proposed Solution

CargoFlow is designed to:

- Organise cargo events into one unified timeline.
- Monitor expected shipment milestones.
- Identify missed milestones and unexpected events.
- Highlight operational bottlenecks and outstanding dependencies.
- Identify the stakeholder expected to take the next action.
- Notify relevant users and track exceptions through resolution.

## Critical Technology Components

1. **Multi-source Data Integration:** Connects operational information from relevant cargo stakeholders.
2. **Cargo Timeline and Event Intelligence:** Organises shipment events and identifies deviations from expected milestones.
3. **Exception Management and Action Engine:** Highlights outstanding issues and directs attention towards the next required action.

## Target Users

- Importers and manufacturers.
- Freight forwarders and clearing organisations.
- Logistics companies.
- Other authorised participants in the port-to-warehouse journey.

## Technology Approach

CargoFlow is designed to work alongside existing industry systems, using APIs and other digital integrations where available, with structured operational data inputs where necessary.

It is not intended to replace Customs, shipping lines, port terminals or existing government systems.

## Current Development Stage

The product concept, intended functionality, initial use cases and proposed technical approach have been defined.

The next milestone is to develop a proof of concept, validate the proposed workflows and test the system with relevant industry participants.

## Project Vision

CargoFlow aims to help Nigerian businesses detect cargo-related problems earlier, coordinate responses more effectively and reduce avoidable logistics delays.
